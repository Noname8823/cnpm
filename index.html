About us

Group 14 - Food Shop

Welcome to Group 14 - Food Shop, where we've been serving freshly made meals since 2024. We take pride in using only the freshest ingredients to create delicious dishes for every occasion. Thank you for choosing us – we look forward to serving you again soon!

Menu

"Honey Glazed Grilled Chicken"

Juicy, tender chicken grilled to perfection and glazed with a sweet and tangy honey sauce. A delightful balance of savory and sweet flavors, perfect for any meal.

"Beef Steak"

A tender, juicy cut of beef, perfectly seared on the outside and cooked to your desired level of doneness. With a rich, savory flavor and a melt-in-your-mouth texture, this steak is the ultimate comfort food, served with a side of your choice for a satisfying meal.

"French Fries"

Crispy on the outside, soft on the inside, these golden French fries are perfectly seasoned and fried to a delightful crunch. A classic and irresistible side dish that pairs well with any meal.

"fried chicken"

Crispy, golden-brown on the outside, tender and juicy on the inside. Our fried chicken is seasoned with a flavorful blend of spices, delivering a crunchy, savory experience in every bite. A timeless favorite for any occasion.

"Pho"

A fragrant and flavorful Vietnamese noodle soup, featuring tender slices of beef or chicken in a savory broth made from simmered bones, herbs, and spices. Served with fresh herbs, lime, and chili, Pho offers a comforting and aromatic experience that warms the soul.

"kimchi soup"

A spicy and savory Korean stew made with fermented kimchi, tofu, vegetables, and pork or beef. The rich, tangy broth brings out the deep flavors of the kimchi, creating a comforting, hearty dish that's perfect for cold weather or anytime you're craving something flavorful and warming.
